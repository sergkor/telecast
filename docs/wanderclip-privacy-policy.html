This Privacy Policy explains how Wanderclip ("we", "us") handles information on the website [SITE URL] (the "Site") and in the Wanderclip publishing application (the "App"). The App is an internal tool we use to publish short travel videos and articles from our own channels to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Telegram and this Site.

1. Information we collect from Site visitors

  • Server logs. Our web host automatically records standard request data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and timestamps. These logs are used only for security and troubleshooting and are deleted on the host's regular schedule.
  • Comments. If comments are enabled and you leave one, we store the name, email address, website and comment text you submit, along with your IP address and browser user agent to help detect spam. Your email address is never shown publicly.
  • Cookies. WordPress may set cookies to remember comment details or, for logged-in editors, to keep a session. We do not use advertising cookies.

2. Embedded content from other websites

Articles on this Site embed videos from YouTube. Embedded content behaves as if you visited YouTube directly: YouTube may collect data about you, set cookies and track your interaction with the embedded player. See the Google Privacy Policy. Links to our Telegram channel and other social profiles take you to those services, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Information the App accesses

The App connects only to accounts owned by Wanderclip. It is not offered to the public and does not access other people's accounts.

  • YouTube (Google). The App requests the youtube.upload permission solely to upload videos, titles and descriptions to our own YouTube channel. It does not read, download, modify or delete any other YouTube data.
  • TikTok. The App uses TikTok's Content Posting API to post videos and captions to our own TikTok account.
  • Pinterest. The App uses the Pinterest API to create Pins on our own boards.
  • Telegram. The App reads public posts from Telegram channels to source content and posts to our own Telegram channel.

OAuth access and refresh tokens for these services are kept only on our private server, are never shared with third parties and are used only to perform the publishing actions described above. Article text may be processed by Google's Gemini API for translation and editing; no personal data of Site visitors is sent to it.

4. Google API Services disclosure

Wanderclip's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use YouTube API Services; by using them you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. You can revoke the App's access to a Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. How we use and share information

We use the information above only to operate, secure and improve the Site and to publish our own content. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Data retention

Comments and their metadata are kept until the comment is deleted. API tokens are kept until access is revoked or the integration is removed, at which point they are deleted from our server.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, export or delete personal data we hold about you (for example, a comment you posted). Depending on where you live, including the EU/UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA), you may have additional rights. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.
